1. Introductions and Expectations

- How much time can each person realistically commit per week?
- What are everyone's strengths?

2. Team Preference Questions (Everyone Answers)
- What type of project excites you most?
- What technologies do you want to learn?
- What technologies do you NOT want to work with?
- Which role interests you most?
    * Frontend
    * Backend
    * Database
    * DevOps
    * Architecture
    * UI/UX
    * Project Management
- Which role interests you least?
- Do you prefer building games, social platforms, tools, AI products, or something else?
- Have you worked with Docker before?
- Have you worked with React/Vue/Svelte before?
- Have you worked with backend frameworks before?
- Are you comfortable presenting during evaluations?
- Would you rather own one large feature or several smaller features?
- What is one thing you absolutely want to learn from this project?
- What is one thing you absolutely want to avoid?
- What part of the evaluation worries you most?
- What part of the project are you most excited about?
