WAYS TO DECIDE IF THE TEAM IS STUCK

1. Elimination Voting
Everyone writes:
- Top 3 ideas
- Bottom 3 ideas
Remove ideas that repeatedly appear in bottom lists.

2. Four-Question Scoring
Rate each idea 1-5:
- Fun
- Technical Interest
- Portfolio Value
- Realistic Completion
Highest score wins.

3. Module Mapping
Before discussing an idea deeply ask:
'Can we reach 14+ points coherently?'

4. Risk Assessment
Ask:
'What could kill this project?'
Compare risks between ideas.

5. Category Voting
Vote on categories before specific projects:
- Gaming
- Social
- Collaboration
- Productivity
- AI
etc.

6. 100 Point Distribution
Each person distributes 100 points among:
- Competitive
- Social
- Casual
- Progression
The combined result shows team priorities.

7. Dream vs Safe Project
Create:
- Dream Project
- Safe Project
Choose a middle ground.

8. Time Estimation
Estimate:
- Best case
- Expected case
- Worst case
Avoid ideas with huge worst-case risks.

9. Majority Vote After Discussion
After every idea has been discussed:
- One anonymous vote
- Highest vote wins
- Team commits to the decision
